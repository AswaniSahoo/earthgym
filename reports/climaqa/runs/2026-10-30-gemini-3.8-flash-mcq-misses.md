## Method

Each miss was read with its question, its four options in the shuffled order the model saw
(`environments/climaqa_v1/climaqa_v1/climaqa_clean_v1.jsonl`), the key and the model's letter,
then put in one bucket:

- **Model error**: the key is right and the model's option is wrong or clearly worse.
- **Ambiguous**: the key and the model's option are both defensible answers to the question as
  written.
- **Flawed item**: the key is wrong, no option is fully right, or the question refers to
  information it does not give.
- **Source only**: the question tests the source document (a book's acknowledgments, a number
  from one text), not climate science.

The sort is one reader's judgment (Claude Code's); no domain expert has checked
it. The baseline review read six of these items (102, 110, 113, 125, 130, 132); its reading
matches this sort on all six.
