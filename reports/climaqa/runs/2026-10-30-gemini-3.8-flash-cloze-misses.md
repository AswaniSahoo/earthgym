## Method

Each wrong reply was read next to its sentence (by id in
`environments/climaqa_v1/climaqa_v1/climaqa_clean_v1.jsonl`) and its key, then put in one bucket:

- **Scorer strictness**: the reply names the same thing as the key in another string (symbol,
  word order, plural or synonym). A looser contract could accept it.
- **Other correct word**: the reply names something else, but the completed sentence is still a
  correct statement and nothing in the sentence rules the reply out. The item has more than one
  answer.
- **Model error**: the completed sentence is wrong.

Eight "other correct word" calls are debatable and marked as such. The sort is one reader's
judgment (Claude Code's), checked against a blind second sort (below). No
domain expert has checked it yet.

## What follows

1. The contract stays as it is. The 8 same-meaning replies would need four different rules. A
   plural rule recovers 1 item. A word-order rule recovers 1 and would make `(scl - senv)` equal
   `(senv - scl)`, the failure docs/log.md records from Phase 1. Symbols and synonyms need per-item
   aliases that a person approves. With no contract change, no decision record or rescore is
   due now.
2. A decision for me: keep exact match and report cloze as exact-word accuracy with this note,
   or approve aliases for the 8 keys after an expert agrees, cut clean set v2, and rescore these
   same logs (103 of 128, 0.805). Neither needs a new paid run.
3. The cloze reward has the same problem. A reward that marks a correct alternative wrong teaches
   a policy to guess the source's word. Before the cloze score is used as a knowledge measure or
   an RL reward, a domain expert should check the 24 other-correct-word items. The two keys that
   are an abbreviation in parentheses, (REML) at 8 and (τa) at 22, are the only two in the 128
   cloze items, the model missed both, and they are the first candidates to drop in v2.

Limits: one model, one run on provider-default sampling, one reader.
