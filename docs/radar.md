Rings: adopt (use now), trial (try in one phase), assess (watch), hold (not now).
Every entry names the date it was checked and the source.

added on 2026-11-01, and the open-model rows (runner, models, fallbacks) on 2026-11-02; the other
rows come from the Phase 2 and 3 checks of 2026-10-30.

or Vertex AI row stays in hold with its facts kept; Groq and OpenRouter are hosted APIs and stay in
